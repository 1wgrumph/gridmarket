#### 4.1.7 Bot diversity and economy draws

**BLUF:** Blends, information limits, bounded learning, and a drawn economy make the population behave like many distinct households rather than seven clones.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Bots, the owner who spawns cohorts, and judges reading the Bots page.
- **What:** Diversity layers 2, 3, 5a, and 6, cohort spawn, and the economy draws (DES-GM-BOTS, DES-GM-ECON).
- **Why:** Diverse behavior makes market activity credible and dormancy meaningful.
- **How:** Dirichlet blends via `gammavariate`, per-bot signal subsets with delay, bias, and noise, and clamped threshold learning.
- **When:** At sampling, after each settled position, and on an admin spawn request.
- **Where:** `population.py`, `economy.py`, `bots_api.py`, and the Bots page.

Each bot blends its main type with 2 others using Dirichlet(6, 1.5, 1.5)
weights, sees only its subset of eight signal families, delayed 0–15
minutes, biased ±10%, and noised 2–10% (AC-GM-BOT-05). After each settled
position each decision threshold moves by at most its learning rate times its
range and is clamped; a restart replays settled positions from the ledger
(AC-GM-BOT-06). Evolution (layer 5b) is dropped and bots are never retired.

Starting cash is lognormal with median $1,000 and sigma 0.8, clamped to
$100–$10,000 and correlated with employment and risk appetite through the
same copula; 65% of bots are employed with lognormal pay of median $40
(AC-GM-ECON-01). An admin request to `POST /v1/admin/bots` adds a cohort of
1–10 bots with an optional seed, capped at 200 bots (AC-GM-BOT-07). The Bots
page shows the dormant rate and, when layers 2, 3, 5a, and 6 are landed,
trait coverage, behavior entropy, and a risk-against-patience scatter;
otherwise it marks them not enabled (AC-GM-UI-07). If S31 is not green by
Sat 14:00 CDT these layers move to phase 2.
