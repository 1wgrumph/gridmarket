### 1.6 Terms and Referenced Documents

**BLUF:** These terms carry one meaning throughout GridMarket, and the referenced documents are the only sources this specification relies on.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Every reader.
- **What:** The vocabulary and the referenced documents.
- **Why:** Market, grid, and process terms overlap; one definition each prevents misreading a requirement.
- **How:** Definitions follow the technical plan's Definitions section.
- **When:** Fixed for revision 0.1.
- **Where:** The technical plan, `design.md`, `CONTRACTS.md`, and the owner intent `gridmarket-intent.html`.

Referenced documents: `gridmarket-technical-plan.md` (requirements, risks,
schedule), `design.md` (design decisions and contracts), `CONTRACTS.md`
(frozen shared names), `gridmarket-intent.html` (owner intent, cited by §
number), and `views/views.json` (system views of the Systems Modeler).
