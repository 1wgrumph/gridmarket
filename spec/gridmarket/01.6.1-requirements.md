#### Requirements

**BLUF:** GridMarket's requirements are Lifecycle-local `AC-GM-*` rows owned by the technical plan, and this specification binds each one to the sections that realize it.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Reviewers and the Test Engineer.
- **What:** Where requirements live and how this document refers to them.
- **Why:** One register avoids two conflicting copies of a requirement.
- **How:** Sections list requirement identifiers in their front matter; Appendix A is generated from them.
- **When:** Fixed at plan approval; changed only by a planning delta.
- **Where:** The technical plan's Requirements section.

No external requirements register exists; the repository was greenfield and
the Lifecycle is not a specification Lifecycle. The requirement statements are
not repeated here, to avoid drift. The table groups them by family.
