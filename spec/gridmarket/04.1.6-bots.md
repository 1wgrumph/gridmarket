#### 4.1.6 Bot population

**BLUF:** Sixty seeded bots of seven types trade through the public API with their own keys, and the whole population is reproducible from one master seed with the standard library alone.

**Frame**

- **Who:** The bots container and the owner who sets the master seed.
- **What:** Bot types, latent traits, households, and the per-bot trading loop (DES-GM-BOTS).
- **Why:** Agent population lens: diverse, reproducible traffic that exercises the real API and risk engine.
- **How:** `population.sample` with `random.Random(f"{master}:{index}")`, a Gaussian copula, and a pure-Python Cholesky factor.
- **When:** At first start on an empty database; each bot then loops on its own schedule.
- **Where:** `population.py`, `bots.py`, and the `bots` container.

When the database is empty the system seeds 60 bots of seven types; with
LoneStar Storage enabled at least 20 of them are its customers
(AC-GM-BOT-02). Seven latent traits (risk appetite, patience, reaction delay,
loss aversion, herd versus contrarian, daily activity, wealth) come from a
Gaussian copula with a fixed target correlation matrix, `statistics.NormalDist`
for the probit, and logistic-normal marginals for bounded traits. Each bot
gets 1 or 2 batteries, lognormal with median 13.5 kWh clamped to 5–40 kWh,
charge and discharge limits of 0.37 × capacity kW clamped to 2–10 kW, a
load zone from fixed weights, a 15–50% reserve habit, and a 24-weight
activity schedule (AC-GM-BOT-04). Two populations from one master seed have
the same SHA-256 digest, and 2,000 samples match the target medians, zone
shares, and rank correlations (AC-GM-BOT-08).

`bots.py` runs one loop per bot through the SDK with a key derived as `gm_`
plus the first 32 URL-safe base64 characters of HMAC-SHA256 of the bot
secret and `bot:<index>`, so the container needs only the secret. Each bot
submits at most 6 orders per rolling 60 s under the same risk controls as any
client (AC-GM-BOT-03), and the seeded traffic submits at least 10 orders per
minute from at least 3 accounts (AC-GM-BOT-01).
