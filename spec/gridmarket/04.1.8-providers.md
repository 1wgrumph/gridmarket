#### 4.1.8 Provider adapters

**BLUF:** The market reaches batteries only through the `ProviderAdapter` protocol, so a second provider company joins the same order books without a market change.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Provider companies (Base simulator, LoneStar Storage) and their customers.
- **What:** The provider seam, heartbeats, and the simulated outage (DES-GM-PROV, CONTRACT-GM-PROVIDER).
- **Why:** Provider seam lens: provider neutrality is the commercial thesis.
- **How:** A registry of enabled adapters; reservations run inside the caller's SQLite transaction.
- **When:** Phase 1 with `base_sim`; phase 2 adds `lonestar` with `GRIDMARKET_LONESTAR=on`.
- **Where:** `providers/`, `health.py`, and the Providers page.

The market reads and reserves capacity only through the adapter interface
(AC-GM-PROV-01); section 4.2.1 lists its operations. `base_sim` serves every
bot while it is the only adapter. With `lonestar` enabled, at least 20
LoneStar Storage customers trade on the same books and the dashboard shows
participant counts for both providers (AC-GM-PROV-02). The health loop has
each enabled adapter record a heartbeat every 10 s. An admin
`POST /v1/admin/providers/lonestar/outage` with `{"active": true}` stops
LoneStar heartbeats until `{"active": false}` or 10 minutes; within 60 s the router
shows the LoneStar health check offline in the alert band (AC-GM-PROV-03).
The Providers page shows, per provider, customers, online assets, heartbeat
age, health probability and band, and outage state (AC-GM-UI-04).
