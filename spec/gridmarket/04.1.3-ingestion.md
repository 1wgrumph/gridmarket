#### 4.1.3 ERCOT and NWS ingestion

**BLUF:** The market polls the ERCOT Worker and NWS within fixed request budgets and serves stale values with their age rather than fail, so a data outage never takes down an endpoint.

**Frame**

- **Who:** The ERCOT and NWS pollers in the app process.
- **What:** Signal ingestion and the signal store (DES-GM-ERCOT, DES-GM-NWS, CONTRACT-GM-SIGNALS).
- **Why:** Data ingestion lens: live data within budget, failing soft.
- **How:** Sliding-window limiters, exponential backoff 5 s doubling to 300 s, and a store keyed by report and zone.
- **When:** Every 5 or 60 minutes per route; NWS forecast hourly and alerts every 5 minutes.
- **Where:** `ercot.py`, `nws.py`, and the `signals` table.

The ERCOT poller reads `/api/snapshot` and the five allowlisted report routes
and stores each value with its settlement-point name, weather zone, load zone,
or constraint identity, its interval, its ERCOT publish time, and its fetch
time (AC-GM-DATA-01). A sliding-window limiter allows at most 12 Worker
requests per 60 s including pagination, and the client never sends `fresh`
(AC-GM-DATA-02). On 401, 404, 429, 5xx, or a connection that fails before a
complete response (20 s timeout), the client backs off from 5 s doubling to
300 s and keeps serving stored values with `stale: true` and `age_s`
(AC-GM-DATA-03).

The NWS client sends a `User-Agent`, resolves each zone's representative
point once, polls the hourly forecast every 60 minutes and active alerts every
5 minutes, and stays within 6 requests per rolling 60 s with the same retry
and stale rule (AC-GM-DATA-05). Load forecasts map weather zones to load
zones with a fixed approximate table. Test fixtures live only under
`backend/tests/fixtures/ercot/`; the live data path has no fixture loader.
