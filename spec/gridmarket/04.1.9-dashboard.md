#### 4.1.9 Dashboard and ERCOT Worker views

**BLUF:** The dashboard is a login-free static client of the same REST API every other client uses, and it links both ways with the Worker's 3D views.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Judges, the owner, and visitors of the 3D views.
- **What:** Dashboard pages, disclosures, theme, and the linked Worker views (DES-GM-UI, DES-GM-VIEWS).
- **Why:** Explainability and onboarding without a second backend.
- **How:** React 19, `@astryxdesign/core` 0.6.0, StyleX, and Recharts 3.10.1, polling `dashboard/src/api.ts` every 2 s.
- **When:** Always served while the app runs; pages land per phase.
- **Where:** `dashboard/` served by FastAPI at `/`; `ercot-hackathon/public/` on the Worker.

The dashboard is a single-page app with no login that refreshes by REST
polling every 2 seconds (AC-GM-UI-01). Its pages are Overview (ERCOT
signals with publish time and staleness, zone scores, participant counts,
market status and anomalies, activity feed, link to the views), Market,
Predictions and router, Providers, Bots with the `/bots/:id` profile,
Judge sandbox, and Spec. The profile page shows traits, blend, household,
job and pay, the cash balance after each fill and deposit in ledger order,
trades, losses, P&L excluding deposits, and dormant status (AC-GM-UI-03).
The shell shows the disclosures that future products are simulated forward
flexibility contracts and not regulated commodity futures, and that a Flex
Credit is not a REC, a cryptocurrency, or a claim on specific electrons
(AC-GM-UI-02). The theme is a dark control-room look with cyan accent and
#5fdd91, #ff7a45, and #f2c14e, plus a light mode; shadcn/ui with Tailwind 4 is
the recorded fallback (AC-GM-UI-06).

The Worker's `/`, `/diagram/`, and `/godseye/` views link to the dashboard.
`/` and `/godseye/` import `market-feed.js`, which reads
`/v1/market/activity` and `/v1/router` by cross-origin GET with a 20 s
timeout; on failure it returns `{offline: true}` and the views keep their
ERCOT content. The Overview links back to the views (AC-GM-EDGE-05).
