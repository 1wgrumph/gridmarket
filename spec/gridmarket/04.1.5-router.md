#### 4.1.5 Decision router

**BLUF:** The decision router answers calibrated probability checks with baseline rules, scores itself with Brier scores, and acts on market state in exactly one case: marking a silent provider offline.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Judges reading the Predictions and router page; the owner during the outage demo.
- **What:** Check families, bands, calibration, and the optional Jev column (DES-GM-ROUTER, CONTRACT-GM-ROUTER).
- **Why:** Explainability and safety: probabilities are shown with their track record and never trade.
- **How:** A registry of check families evaluated every 60 s; results stored in `router_results`.
- **When:** Every 60 s; outcomes resolve when each horizon ends.
- **Where:** `decision_router.py`, `health.py`, `jev.py`, served at `GET /v1/router`.

The market family answers the DART spread check per zone and future delivery
hour: p = logistic(k × (score − 50) / 50 + b × sign(DA − last RT)) with
k = 2.0 and b = 0.3, horizon the end of the delivery hour, and outcome 1 when
the hourly RT average exceeds DA. `GET /v1/router` reports each check's band
and its Brier score over resolved results (AC-GM-ROUTER-01). The health family
(phase 2) estimates, for the ERCOT Worker and each provider, the probability
of becoming unavailable within 15 minutes from error rate, 429 rate, p95
latency, snapshot age, and heartbeat age (AC-GM-ROUTER-03).

Below 0.50 a result is logged for calibration, from 0.50 to below 0.80 it is
shown for review, and at 0.80 or above it raises an alert event. The router
holds no account key and submits no order (AC-GM-ROUTER-02). Its only acting
baseline rule marks a provider offline when its last heartbeat is older than
30 s; new sells from that provider's customers are then rejected with
`PROVIDER_OFFLINE` until the next heartbeat (AC-GM-PROV-04). With
`GRIDMARKET_JEV=on` the router adds the hosted Jev probability as a separate
column that changes no state; otherwise it makes no Jev call
(AC-GM-ROUTER-04).
