#### Baselines

**BLUF:** Four named baselines anchor GridMarket: the planning package, the contract skeleton, the ERCOT Worker source, and the Worker snapshot shape.

**Frame**

- **Who:** Anyone who reproduces or reviews a build.
- **What:** The fixed commits and documents that later work builds on.
- **Why:** Parallel lanes need one frozen set of names to stay compatible (RISK-GM-05, RISK-GM-19).
- **How:** Record each baseline by path or commit; amend only through the recorded procedure.
- **When:** Frozen at wave 1; amended only by dated entries.
- **Where:** The plan directory, `CONTRACTS.md`, and `ercot-hackathon/`.

The table lists each baseline, what it fixes, and how it may change.
