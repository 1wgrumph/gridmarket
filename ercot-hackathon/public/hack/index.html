// THE PROBLEM

Texas doesn't get into trouble at the peak. It gets into trouble after sunset.

On 26 August 2026, demand peaked at 90.5 GW at 4:30 PM and prices stayed calm. At 10:15 PM, with demand down to 73.5 GW, the Houston hub cleared at $780.46/MWh, about eight times its day-ahead price. Solar had gone from 16.9 GW to zero. Every big Houston spike from June to September landed between 8 and 11 PM.

01 · the peak is the wrong target

Batteries that discharge at the afternoon peak spend themselves before the hours that actually cost money.

02 · timing beats size

In the replay, 1,000 batteries ran down to reserve nearly two hours before the spike. More capacity wouldn't have fixed that; better timing would.

03 · the data is public, the market isn't

Trading ERCOT takes QSE registration and credit. GridMarket lets anyone test strategies on real conditions with nothing real at stake.

04 · people and bots need one rulebook

Every order goes through the same boundary and the same checks, whether a person or a bot places it.

// THE LOOP

Six steps. One day on the grid.

Solar charges the fleet at midday, the evening ramp creates the need, the score finds the hours, credits price them, orders fill them, and settlement pays out. Replay any day and break it.

01 · MIDDAY
solar charges

Fill up cheap

Batteries charge 10 AM–4 PM while prices sit under $60 and solar is at its highest.

02 · EVENING
net-load ramp

The sun sets

Solar drops toward zero, load stays high, and net load climbs into the hours ERCOT struggles with.

03 · SCORE
zone scarcity

Find the hours

Each zone-hour gets a 0–100 score from net load and day-ahead price. Runs of 70+ are help windows.

04 · FLEX CREDIT
zone + hour

Price it

Credits are tied to a zone and an hour, so the exchange prices the evening, not the day.

05 · ORDER
same API for all

Trade it

People, the Judge sandbox and 60 bots go through one order boundary with the same risk checks.

06 · SETTLE
simulated payout

Pay it out

Delivered flexibility settles against what the grid actually did. No real money or energy moves.

// TECHNOLOGY STACK

Real data in, simulated money out.

Everything the market reacts to is real ERCOT data. Everything tradable is simulated and labelled that way.

ERCOT Public API · the truth

Hub and zone prices, day-ahead prices, SCED lambda, demand, wind and solar forecasts, weather assumptions and zone load forecasts.

Cloudflare Workers · the data edge

Token cached in KV, sequential calls with 429 backoff, cached snapshots. Worker Assets serve God's Eye and the diagrams from the same URL as the API.

EIA-860 · the map

1,023 Texas plants from the 2025 early release, with LMP node designations matched to ERCOT settlement points.

CesiumJS · the globe

Keyless Esri imagery, fuel-type silhouettes, battery point clouds and zone help rings. GSAP drives the replay and tour.

FastAPI + SQLite · the exchange

Orders, providers, credits and settlement, with a React dashboard, Docker Compose and around 350 tests.

Jev · the decisions

Calibrated checks over structured ERCOT data, routed by confidence to alert, review or log, and scored against outcomes.
