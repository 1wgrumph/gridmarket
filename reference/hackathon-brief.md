## Tracks

Teams pick one track; one project may be entered into up to 2 tracks if it fits.

1. **Open Grid Data** — ERCOT publishes prices, load, generation, congestion.
   Base's business runs on reading it better than everyone else. Build on it.
   Show what you can see in the data that most people miss.
2. **Orchestration** — Build a system that coordinates many independent things
   (agents, jobs, workers). What matters is how it holds up when pieces fail.
3. **Most Commercializable** — Base is a power company, not a battery company,
   building a world-class product for its members. Build something that could
   actually ship as a product on top of what Base does. Show off your taste.

## Judging (100 points)

Philosophy: real, working systems — not slide decks or simple API wrappers.
Judged off the 5-minute demo video and the codebase.

1. Technical Execution & Completeness (30)
   - 15 Completeness: core workflow completes without crashing.
   - 15 Technical Depth: real engineering, a complex pipeline, not a basic wrapper.
2. Fit to the Track (30)
   - 15 The Problem: meaningfully attacks the track's problem set.
   - 15 The "Why": team can articulate why the approach solves a meaningful problem.
3. Value & Impact (20)
   - 10 Insight Quality: output is non-obvious and genuinely useful.
   - 10 Usability: Base or another user could use it tomorrow.
4. Innovation and Execution (20)
   - 10 Creativity: novel combination of tools or data; looks and feels good.
   - 10 Performance: optimized for speed or scale.
